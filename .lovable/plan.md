# Local SEO, AI visibility, readability, and performance plan

## Goal
Strengthen CAR & AUTO TRAVELS' visibility for people seeking local car, taxi, auto, pickup/drop, and outstation trips around Madugula, Anakapalli, and Visakhapatnam. Improve readability and smoothness without changing the white theme or inventing business claims. Search ranking cannot be guaranteed; Google and AI platforms decide what they show.

## Work
1. **Polish the existing pages:** Correct the homepage heading spacing seen in the published page, make each service page's wording and headings clear and locally relevant, and strengthen helpful links between services, areas, FAQs, reviews, and contact. Keep existing routes and working features.
2. **Check colors and mobile/desktop usability:** Verify actual text, link, button, and expanded-menu contrast at phone and desktop sizes. Keep the softened palette and adjust only any elements that fail readability checks.
3. **Improve smoothness based on evidence:** Review hero video/photo loading, image delivery, and page responsiveness; make focused improvements only where measurements show a benefit. Current real-visitor samples are too small to declare a page slow (homepage readings are in single digits to ten per metric); re-check after changes.
4. **Strengthen accurate search and AI signals:** Review each public page's title, description, canonical/social metadata, business/service structured data, sitemap, robots rules, and `/llms.txt`; correct any mismatch and keep all claims consistent with verified site content. Use relevant service/location phrases naturally in useful page copy rather than stuffing keyword tags.
5. **Local discovery and measurement:** Check whether the business already has a Google Business Profile before any setup, avoiding duplicate listings. Once the owner connects Google Search Console, use its verified property to monitor Google's indexing and search queries; submit the sitemap if needed. Consider a custom business domain as a later trust and consistency improvement; no domain will be purchased or connected without separate approval.
6. **Verify the result:** Re-check key published pages, service-menu behavior, readable text and controls at phone and desktop sizes, and the public crawl files. Re-run the SEO foundations review after changes and report what is verified versus what needs owner access or time.

## Current evidence
- The latest SEO foundations review is current and passes checks for homepage reachability, server-rendered pages, AI-readable markdown, crawler rules, sitemap, favicon, and homepage metadata/social preview.
- The published homepage, Contact, Areas, Car Travel, and Auto Travel pages each returned HTTP 200 in a live check. The rendered home heading joins “Auto” and “Travel” without a space and should be corrected.
- The public site already has page metadata, business/service structured data, internal links, a sitemap, `robots.txt`, and `llms.txt`; preserve and refine these rather than replacing them.
- Search Console is not connected, and the owner is unsure whether a Google Business Profile exists. Google's current indexing and ranking position are therefore unverified.
- Analytics recorded 34 visits and 53 page views from 2026-10-15 to 2026-11-13. One visit was attributed to Google; these small counts do not establish rankings or customer bookings.
- Recent Core Web Vitals readings are sparse (generally 1–10 per metric/page), below the 30-reading threshold for calling a page slow. Semrush returned no data for the domain and sample local keywords, so keyword demand and organic rankings remain unknown.
- Search results show other taxi providers target local/outstation and route-specific searches, but do not establish this business's prices, availability, ratings, or differentiators.

## Not included
- No promise of a first-place Google ranking, fabricated reviews/ratings, unsupported prices or claims, keyword stuffing, paid campaigns, or creation of a duplicate business listing.
- Broad social-media and paid-ad campaigns are deferred because the selected priority was Google Search and AI visibility.
